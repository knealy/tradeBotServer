## ✅ Trend Scalping Strategy - Market Order Fix

**Issue:** Strategy was using current price for stop entry orders, causing "Order price is outside allowed range" errors.

**Root Cause:** 
- `place_bracket_order()` uses stop entry orders (BUY STOP must be above market, SELL STOP below)
- Strategy was passing `current_price` as entry, which is neither above nor below market
- Invalid for stop orders

**Fix:**
- Changed execution to use `place_native_bracket_order()` instead
- This places MARKET order with immediate SL/TP brackets
- Perfect for scalping where you want immediate fills
- Entry at best available price (true market order)

**Files Modified:**
- `strategies/trend_scalping_strategy.py` - execute() method updated

**Test it now:**
```bash
python core/strategy_executor.py --account_select=1 --strategy=trend_scalping --symbols=mnq
```

### Usage:
