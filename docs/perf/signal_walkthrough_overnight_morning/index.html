Do body_reversion and morning_range_reversion overlap?

Yes, in clock time they can. Shipped TOML gives body_reversion a wide executor window (start_time=00:00 / end_time=23:59 US/Eastern in config/strategies/body_reversion.toml) with rth_only=false by default, so a big-body 5m bar can signal anytime. morning_range_reversion builds its anchor from 5m bars whose opens fall in 07:00–08:00 ET, then arms fades on sweeps after that window (config/strategies/morning_range_reversion.toml, module docstring in strategies/morning_range_reversion_strategy.py). So you routinely get morning activity 08:00–late morning ET while body signals can still fire on the same session date on other 5m bars.

What happens typically: strategies do not share signal state. On the same symbol/account, the next signal usually loses to risk / position limits (max_positions, pending brackets, executor gates) if the first strategy already has exposure—there is no merge of the two edges; you get whichever path the bot executes first plus cooldown / max-daily-trade caps per strategy config.
